Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for business founders, directors, and operational heads.

Can we engage isdone for only one specific service?

Yes. While our unified five-domain model provides the highest operational synergy, you can start with any single domain (e.g., only HR & payroll compliance, or only custom CRM development) and expand as your business demands.

How do you ensure payroll and statutory compliance without error?

Our HR specialists maintain strict calculation frameworks for ESI, PF, LWF, and TDS, adhering to the latest statutory guidelines with comprehensive audit trails before filing so you never face penalties.

What is your approach to proprietary software & data security?

All client source code, customer records, and operational data are protected under strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). We employ encrypted repositories, role-based access, and cyber security best practices across all builds.
